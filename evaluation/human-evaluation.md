# Double-blind writing evaluation

For each selected section, prepare three anonymized outputs: original author text, an ordinary LLM continuation, and FastWrite evidence-aware output. Randomize labels independently per evaluator.

Evaluators score each output from 1–5 on correctness, argument completeness, concision and citation accuracy. Record unsupported claims separately. Operational metrics include accepted hunk ratio, post-accept rollback ratio, manual edit distance and revision rounds. At least two evaluators score every item; disagreements of two or more points require adjudication. Preserve task prompts, evidence boundaries, anonymization seed and aggregate results, but do not publish manuscript text without authorization.
