# Artificial intelligence

Read `references/profile.md` and then the selected file in `references/venues/`. Treat venue hard constraints as non-negotiable and do not fabricate experiments, citations, data, results, or compliance status.

## Workflow

1. Identify the target venue, edition, track, and manuscript stage.
2. Turn every applicable hard constraint into a verifiable task; report rules not verified for the selected edition as uncertain.
3. Keep central evidence in the main paper and make contributions traceable to proofs, data, or experiments.
4. Preserve LaTex, labels, citation keys, and factual scope during revision.
5. Review against manuscript evidence and venue criteria; distinguish blocking defects from suggestions.

## Citation integrity

Never add a citation unless its bibliographic record is supplied or can be verified by an authoritative identifier or publisher record. Check that every cited work exists, matches its authors/title/year/venue/DOI or arXiv identifier, is not retracted when that status is known, and actually supports the nearby claim. Report unverifiable, malformed, misattributed, duplicate, or claim-mismatched references as blockers.
