# AAAI-27 Main Technical Track

This is the next officially announced AAAI edition as of the verification date.

## Hard constraints

- Submit an anonymous, double-blind, US-Letter PDF in the AAAI-27 two-column camera-ready style. Remove author, affiliation, acknowledgements, and other identifying material.
- The main paper is at most 9 pages: 7 pages of non-reference content and pages 8--9 exclusively for references. Figures, tables, and any ethics discussion in the main text count against the 7 content pages.
- Complete and submit the AAAI-27 reproducibility checklist separately. Supporting code, data, and supplementary material may be provided, but evaluation-critical content belongs in the main paper.
- Do not submit work that is simultaneously under review, accepted, or published at another archival venue.

## Writing and review priorities

- Make the contribution substantive, original, technically sound, relevant to a broad AI audience, and clear rather than narrowly incremental.
- Support theoretical or empirical claims with enough detail for reproducibility; document experiments, proofs, data, and code where relevant.
- Address responsible-research issues, including sensitive data, human-subject concerns, and data or algorithmic bias when relevant.

## Planning checklist

- Reserve the seven content pages for problem, contribution, method, evidence, and any necessary ethical discussion; reserve pages 8--9 only for references.
- Prepare an anonymization pass over text, PDF metadata, links, and supplementary files.
- Map every reproducibility-checklist response to a concrete manuscript section or supplied artifact.
- Identify the strongest broad-AI contribution and the evidence that validates it before drafting the abstract and introduction.
