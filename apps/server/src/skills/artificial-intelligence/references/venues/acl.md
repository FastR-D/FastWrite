# ACL 2026 Main Conference

ACL 2027 requirements were not officially published at the verification date; these are the latest verified ACL main-conference requirements and ARR policies.

## Hard constraints

- Submit through ACL Rolling Review using the ACL/ARR format and two-way anonymized review. Remove names, affiliations, acknowledgements, identifying self-references, and identifying or trackable repository links from the paper and supplementary material.
- Choose either a Long Paper (8 pages) or Short Paper (4 pages). Ethical considerations and limitations are excluded from this content limit when placed at the end; accepted final versions receive one additional content page.
- Include a Limitations section and complete the Responsible NLP Research Checklist in the ARR submission form with specific section references or justified answers. Incorrect, incomplete, or misleading checklist responses can be desk-rejected.
- Keep appendices in the required double-column format unless an approved exception applies.

## Writing and review priorities

- Establish a substantial, original, unpublished contribution to computational linguistics or NLP; negative results, resources, surveys, position papers, and replications are in scope when their contribution is clear.
- Treat linguistic setting, languages, data provenance, evaluation design, limitations, risks, and reproducibility as first-class evidence.
- Make claims commensurate with the languages, populations, tasks, and datasets actually evaluated; distinguish empirical observation from general claims.

## Planning checklist

- Select Long or Short before allocating sections and reserve end matter for limitations and ethical considerations.
- Audit anonymity in prose, citations, PDF metadata, repositories, supplements, and acknowledgements.
- Complete the Responsible NLP checklist early and link each applicable answer to manuscript evidence or a justified omission.
- Specify data licenses/provenance, language coverage, evaluation protocol, and credible limitations before claiming generality.
