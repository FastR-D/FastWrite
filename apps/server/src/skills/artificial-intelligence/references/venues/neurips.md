# NeurIPS 2026 Main Track

## Hard constraints

- Use the NeurIPS 2026 LaTeX style file and submit one PDF. The main text is limited to 9 content pages, including figures and tables; references, technical appendices, and the mandatory paper checklist do not count toward that limit.
- The submission and supporting material must preserve double-blind anonymity. Submit the required NeurIPS paper checklist in the combined PDF; separately supplied code/data must be anonymized and directly support the paper.
- Select the submission's contribution type. The accepted camera-ready version receives one additional content page.
- Do not use hidden instructions or prompt injection intended to influence reviewers or tools.

## Writing and review priorities

- State claims precisely and support them with reproducible experiments and/or sound theoretical analysis, positioned against relevant prior work.
- Write for the selected contribution type: General, Theory, Use-Inspired, Concept & Feasibility, or Negative Results. Quality, clarity, significance, and originality are interpreted in that context.
- Be candid about limitations and potential negative societal impacts; an explicit “broader impacts” section is not required, but the required checklist covers responsible research, transparency, ethics, and societal impact.

## Planning checklist

- Choose and justify the contribution type before deciding the paper narrative and evaluation standard.
- Budget no more than nine content pages; place proofs and extra experimental detail in appendices only when the main evidence remains understandable.
- Complete every paper-checklist item with section-level evidence or an honest justification.
- Anonymize the PDF, references to prior work, code/data archive, and all supplementary metadata.
