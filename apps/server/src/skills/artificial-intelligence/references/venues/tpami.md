# IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI)

## Hard constraints

- Use the current IEEE Computer Society / TPAMI manuscript template and numbered IEEE reference style. Supply the complete manuscript as requested by the IEEE Author Portal.
- Submit original work only; a journal extension of a conference paper must be materially extended and must disclose and cite the earlier version according to the live TPAMI instructions.
- The journal's permitted length depends on manuscript type and the current IEEE policy. Do not treat a page count inferred from another IEEE journal as authoritative; select the type in the portal and re-check the current TPAMI limit and any mandatory overlength charge before submission.
- Observe IEEE publication ethics, permissions for reused material, and any required author identifiers or declarations in the live portal.
- This is a journal submission, not an anonymous conference track: prepare author and affiliation information when the current TPAMI workflow requests it; do not apply double-blind masking unless the live venue instruction explicitly requires it.

## Writing and review priorities

- Establish a durable contribution in pattern analysis, computer vision, or machine intelligence, with breadth and depth beyond a lightly expanded conference paper.
- Define the task, assumptions, method, and evaluation protocol precisely enough for independent assessment.
- Compare against strong relevant methods under fair settings; separate gains caused by the proposed method from implementation, data, or tuning effects.
- Include diagnostic analysis, ablations, failure cases, and limitations where they are needed to support the central claim.
- Connect theory and practice: explain why formal results, design choices, or empirical findings change the field's understanding.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Choose the TPAMI manuscript type before setting a page budget; verify its current limit in the official portal.
- [ ] Convert the source to the current IEEE template without changing margins, font size, or bibliography layout to gain space.
- [ ] Build an explicit comparison to the closest conference or prior-work version, including new substance and proper citation.
- [ ] Audit experiments for matched data splits, preprocessing, compute budget, baselines, and statistical reporting.
- [ ] Prepare reproducible code/data or a justified availability statement and complete all portal declarations.
