# ICCV 2025 Main Conference

ICCV 2027 requirements were not officially published at the verification date; these are the latest verified ICCV main-conference requirements.

## Hard constraints

- Use the ICCV 2025 author-kit style. The paper has at most 8 pages including figures and tables; additional pages may contain only cited references.
- Submissions and supplementary material must be properly anonymized for double-blind review. A paper that is over length, uses the wrong template, or is improperly anonymized can be rejected without review.
- Submit supplementary material by the same deadline as the paper when applicable.
- Follow the conference ethics guidance and do not make submission changes outside the published process.

## Writing and review priorities

- Present a clear computer-vision problem, a technically differentiated method, and evidence that isolates the source of improvement.
- Pair quantitative comparisons with informative visual analysis, ablations, robustness or failure cases, and reproducible dataset and implementation detail.
- Discuss relevant data, privacy, bias, consent, and deployment risks rather than implying that a benchmark score alone proves practical suitability.

## Planning checklist

- Use the eight content pages for problem, method, results, and visual diagnostics; keep reference pages reference-only.
- Plan anonymization of text, citations, supplements, metadata, and links before preparing the final PDF.
- Predefine datasets, splits, metrics, baselines, ablations, compute/reporting detail, and failure-case visualizations.
- Recheck the forthcoming ICCV edition before using this file for a new submission.
