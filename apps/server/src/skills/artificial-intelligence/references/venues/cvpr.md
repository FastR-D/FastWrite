# CVPR 2026 Main Conference

## Hard constraints

- Use the CVPR 2026 submission template. The paper has at most 8 pages including figures and tables; additional pages may contain only cited references.
- Submissions are double blind. Properly anonymize the paper and supplementary material; do not rely on external links that expand content or can compromise anonymity.
- A paper that exceeds the content limit, omits the required template, or is improperly anonymized can be rejected without review.
- Do not include hidden text or instructions intended to influence reviewers or automated tools.

## Writing and review priorities

- Make the visual problem, technical contribution, experimental protocol, and measured improvement easy to verify from the main paper.
- Use appropriate visual baselines and controlled ablations; report limitations, failure cases, and evaluation boundaries rather than relying only on qualitative examples.
- Address applicable research-ethics concerns, including data, human subjects, bias, privacy, and potentially harmful uses.

## Planning checklist

- Allocate the eight content pages across task definition, method, quantitative evidence, ablations, and diagnostic visualizations; reserve references for reference-only pages.
- Verify that every figure, table, caption, PDF property, link, supplement, and repository remains anonymous and self-contained.
- Define evaluation metrics, datasets/splits, comparison protocol, and failure-case analysis before drafting claims of visual-state-of-the-art performance.
- Run a final template, page-limit, and anonymity check before submission.
