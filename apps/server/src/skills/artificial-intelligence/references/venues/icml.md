# ICML 2026 Main Track

## Hard constraints

- Use the ICML 2026 style files and submit one anonymous PDF for double-blind review. The main paper is limited to 8 pages; references, the required impact statement, and appendices are unlimited. Accepted papers receive one additional main-paper page.
- Put a required impact statement at the end of the paper, before references, addressing ethical aspects and potential societal consequences.
- All claims must be supported by reproducible experiments and/or sound theoretical analysis. Conforming to the published format and policies is required to avoid rejection without review.
- At least one qualified author per submission must agree to reciprocal reviewing unless an official exception applies; do not use prompt injection.

## Writing and review priorities

- Frame original, rigorous work of significant interest to the machine-learning community, and locate it accurately in prior research.
- Make assumptions, theory, experimental design, implementation details, uncertainty, and scope of evidence inspectable.
- Treat broader impact, ethics, limitations, and future societal consequences as part of the research argument, not boilerplate.

## Planning checklist

- Constrain the core argument to eight pages; move supporting proofs and detailed experiments to appendices without making central evidence inaccessible.
- Draft the separate impact statement before final page allocation and ensure it addresses the actual risks or explains why none need emphasis.
- Verify double-blind anonymity across PDF metadata, citations, links, code, and appendices.
- Link every central claim to a reproducible experiment or theorem and an appropriate baseline or related-work comparison.
