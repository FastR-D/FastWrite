# International Journal of Computer Vision (IJCV)

## Hard constraints

- Submit original work that is not simultaneously under consideration elsewhere and has approval from all authors and responsible institutions.
- Follow the current Springer Nature / IJCV submission guidelines for editable source, title-page information, declarations, permissions, supplementary material, and the selected article type.
- Use the current Springer Nature journal template when preparing the manuscript files requested by the submission system; do not alter its typography or geometry to simulate extra space.
- The official IJCV submission-guidelines page does not state one journal-wide hard page limit. Set a content-driven budget and re-check the live journal instructions and article type before submission.
- Provide disclosures required by the live Springer workflow, including competing interests, funding, data/code availability where applicable, and permissions for third-party material.

## Writing and review priorities

- Make the computer-vision problem, novelty, and relationship to prior work clear to readers beyond the immediate benchmark or dataset.
- Give enough detail to reproduce data construction, training or estimation, implementation choices, evaluation, and visual results.
- Use fair, well-motivated comparisons; distinguish accuracy gains from protocol choices, extra data, compute, or post-processing.
- Pair quantitative results with analysis of what the method learns, when it fails, and which assumptions bound its use.
- For journal extensions, add genuine depth—new analysis, theory, data, validation, or insight—not only additional experiments.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Confirm that the manuscript fits IJCV's scope and choose the article type in the live submission system.
- [ ] Rebuild figures and tables for legibility at publication scale and obtain permissions for any reused visual material.
- [ ] Specify datasets, splits, preprocessing, model selection, training details, metrics, and uncertainty.
- [ ] Compare with the most relevant vision literature and state the substantive difference from any conference version.
- [ ] Complete Springer declarations and attach source, supplementary files, data/code links, or an availability rationale.
