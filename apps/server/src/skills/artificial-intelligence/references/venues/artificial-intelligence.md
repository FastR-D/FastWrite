# Artificial Intelligence (AIJ)

## Hard constraints

- Submit original work that is not under review or published elsewhere; disclose any closely related prior publication or submission.
- Write in English. For review, use a readable single-column manuscript on A4 or US Letter paper with at least 11-point text; do not submit a compressed conference-style double-column manuscript.
- Include the complete article material in the review manuscript, including an abstract, references, and publication-quality illustrations.
- Follow the live Elsevier Guide for Authors for title-page metadata, competing-interest and funding declarations, data availability, and any required disclosure of generative-AI assistance. These workflow fields can change independently of the journal's scholarly criteria.
- No fixed journal-wide manuscript page limit is stated in the official guide used here. Plan for a complete but economical article and re-check the live guide and the selected article type before submission.

## Writing and review priorities

- Make the advance significant for AI as a field, rather than only an engineering application or incremental benchmark improvement.
- Give a complete account of the problem, prior literature, method, proofs or empirical evidence, and the motivations and implications of the result.
- For empirical work, make data, protocols, baselines, metrics, uncertainty, and limitations sufficient for an expert to assess the claimed advance.
- For theoretical work, state assumptions and formal results precisely, include essential proofs, and explain their relevance to AI.
- Keep the narrative journal-complete: use the available space to explain why the result matters and how it connects to the broader literature.
