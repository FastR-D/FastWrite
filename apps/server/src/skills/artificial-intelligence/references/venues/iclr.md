# ICLR 2026

## Hard constraints

- Submission main text is at most 9 pages; references and appendices are excluded. Discussion revisions and camera-ready papers may use 10 main-text pages.
- Use the official ICLR 2026 style and preserve double-blind anonymity in the paper and supplement. Identity disclosure can cause desk rejection.
- Do not submit substantially similar work in parallel. Keep third-person self-citations complete, and anonymize supplementary code and links.
- Authors retain responsibility for all AI-assisted content. Disclose significant LLM involvement in research ideation or writing in the prescribed appendix section.

## Writing and review priorities

- Make the learning problem, assumptions, baselines, ablations, uncertainty, compute/data provenance, limitations, and reproducibility evidence explicit.
- Keep claims within evaluated datasets and settings. An ethics statement is recommended when the work raises relevant issues, and a short reproducibility statement is strongly encouraged.
- Treat references as evidence: verify bibliographic identity and ensure every cited source supports the adjacent claim.

## Planning checklist

- Reserve no more than 9 submission pages for main text and keep references/appendices after it.
- Check the `iclr2026` style token, author anonymity, affiliations, acknowledgments, funding identifiers, repository metadata, and self-citation wording.
- Plan reproducibility, ethics where applicable, limitations, and any significant-LLM-use disclosure before final submission.
