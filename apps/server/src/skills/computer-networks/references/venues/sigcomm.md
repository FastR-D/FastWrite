# ACM SIGCOMM

## Hard constraints

- Follow the live SIGCOMM main-track CFP, ACM template, and current submission system; exact page, anonymity, artifact, and appendix rules vary by year and must be rechecked.
- The CCF-covered form is the full main-track research paper, not a workshop, poster, demo, or short paper.
- Preserve current double-blind, conflict, supplementary-material, ethics, and artifact requirements.

## Writing and review priorities

- Show a significant networking contribution using a clear problem setting, sound design, credible implementation/analysis, and convincing evaluation.
- Make trace provenance, testbed realism, topology, baselines, and deployment limitations explicit.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Capture the selected year's page budget/template and anonymous-review rules.
- [ ] Include fair baseline and ablation/sensitivity evidence.
- [ ] Verify all cited protocols, standards, traces, and related-work claims.
