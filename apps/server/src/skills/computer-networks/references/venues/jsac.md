# IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications

## Hard constraints

- Follow the current IEEE Author Center and the selected JSAC issue's author instructions, template, rights, and submission workflow.
- Length, article type, anonymity, and mandatory material may differ by special issue; verify them in the live call and portal rather than inferring a fixed cap.
- Disclose prior versions and comply with the active ethics, data, and authorship policies.

## Writing and review priorities

- Fit the paper to the selected communications area and substantiate models, protocol design, measurements, and trade-offs.
- Provide fair comparisons and sufficient reproducibility for claimed gains.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Identify the special issue or regular-paper route and confirm its current constraints.
- [ ] Preserve trace/data provenance and privacy disclosures.
- [ ] Validate every standard, RFC, paper, DOI, and quantitative related-work claim.
