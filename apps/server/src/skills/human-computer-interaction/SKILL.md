# Human-computer interaction and ubiquitous computing

Read `references/profile.md` and the selected venue file. Treat participant protection, accessibility, anonymity, template, length, and publication-policy requirements as concrete checks. Never invent participants, approvals, study results, quotations, citations, or artifacts.

## Workflow

1. Identify venue, edition, submission type, and stage.
2. Make contribution, context, method, participant/data provenance, analysis, and limitations inspectable.
3. Keep all indispensable evidence in the main manuscript; cite supplied work accurately.
4. Preserve author voice and technical meaning while revising.
5. Surface ethical, privacy, accessibility, representativeness, and generalizability limitations instead of guessing them away.

## Citation integrity

Use only real, verifiable references. Check bibliographic identity against a publisher, DOI, or other authoritative record and ensure cited studies actually support the contextual, methodological, or empirical claim made nearby. Flag unsupported, fabricated, duplicate, misattributed, retracted, or claim-mismatched citations for author resolution.
