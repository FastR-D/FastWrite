# UIST 2026 Papers

## Hard constraints

- Submit original, anonymous work in the required two-column format. Standard papers are at most 10 pages and short papers at most 5 pages; references and appendices do not count, while acknowledgements do.
- The main paper must stand alone; supporting appendices are permitted but cannot substitute for a complete contribution.
- Follow the current author guide for novelty, anonymity, accessibility, AI-assisted writing, and ACM publication policies. The CFP asks authors to avoid using AI agents to generate content, while allowing editing/checking of the author's own text.
- Accepted papers may add up to 10% to address reviewer requests; this is a camera-ready allowance, not a submission budget.

## Writing and review priorities

- State the problem/opportunity, specific prior-work comparison, technical/design innovation, and evidence that it works.
- Make interaction experience, evaluation protocol, accessibility, and limitations concrete.

## Planning checklist

- Select the 10-page standard or 5-page short form before outlining.
- Blind text, metadata, supplements, code, and videos.
- Ensure the central user-interface contribution is complete in the main body.
