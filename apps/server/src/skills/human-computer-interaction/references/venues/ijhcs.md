# International Journal of Human-Computer Studies

## Hard constraints

- Submit original work in English through the current Elsevier workflow and supply the current manuscript, metadata, declarations, permissions, and supplementary files requested there.
- Follow the live guide for authors for template, title-page, competing-interest, funding, data-availability, and generative-AI disclosure requirements.
- No current official guide was available in the verified source that establishes a journal-wide hard page limit; set the budget only after confirming the selected article type in the live guide.

## Writing and review priorities

- Explain the human-computer problem, contribution, method, evidence, and implications beyond a narrow interface demonstration.
- Make study context, recruitment/data provenance, analysis, limitations, and ethical safeguards inspectable.

## Planning checklist

- Confirm the live article type and its length/format requirements.
- Obtain permissions and complete all Elsevier declarations.
- Keep inferences commensurate with the people, contexts, and data actually studied.
