# ACM Transactions on Computer-Human Interaction

## Hard constraints

- Submit original work through the current ACM workflow and follow the current ACM submission template and publication policies; do not alter generated layout to manufacture space.
- Obtain permissions for third-party material and follow ACM authorship, research-with-human-participants, conflict, and publication-ethics policies.
- The official ACM-wide submission guidance used here does not state a TOCHI-wide hard page limit or a journal-specific anonymous-review rule; verify the current TOCHI submission portal before finalizing those settings.

## Writing and review priorities

- Make the HCI contribution, user/context, method, evidence, and design implications intelligible and auditable.
- Report participant/data provenance, study protocol, analysis, limitations, accessibility, and ethics appropriate to the evidence.

## Planning checklist

- Confirm current TOCHI article type, review mode, and any venue-specific length policy in the live portal.
- Check consent, privacy, participant protection, accessibility, and permissions before submission.
- Trace every design or empirical claim to reported evidence; do not invent participants, quotations, or citations.
