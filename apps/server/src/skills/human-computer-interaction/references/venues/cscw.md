# CSCW 2026 Papers

## Hard constraints

- Use the ACM/PACMHCI `acmsmall` submission format; a completely different template is desk-rejected.
- The current call does not set a fixed page limit. Papers under 5,000 or over 12,000 words receive additional scrutiny, and a length incommensurate with contribution can be desk-rejected.
- Submit novel work focused on social, cooperative, collaborative, or social-computing aspects; individual-user-only, uncontextualized algorithmic, or systems work is out of scope.
- The source does not explicitly confirm anonymous review, so do not impose a blind-submission check without rechecking the active submission portal.

## Writing and review priorities

- Situate the work in CSCW concepts or theories and make its social/collective contribution explicit.
- Support designs, systems, and empirical claims with context-sensitive human-centered evidence.

## Planning checklist

- Explain social technology mediation and CSCW relevance in the introduction.
- Keep length proportional to contribution and ensure the submission uses `acmsmall`.
- Check the active portal for review mode, author fields, and ethics/participant requirements.
