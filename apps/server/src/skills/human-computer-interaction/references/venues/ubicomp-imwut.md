# UbiComp/IMWUT 2026

## Hard constraints

- The 2026 main technical program presents eligible IMWUT papers from IMWUT 2025 Issue 4 and IMWUT 2026 Issues 1--3; submit through the current IMWUT journal instructions rather than a separate UbiComp paper CFP.
- The verified UbiComp page delegates detailed submission and review rules to IMWUT and does not itself confirm a fixed page limit, review anonymity, or LaTeX token. Recheck the live IMWUT journal instructions before enforcing them.

## Writing and review priorities

- Connect design, development, or deployment of ubiquitous/pervasive technology to human experiences and social impacts.
- Make systems, sensing, deployment context, data, and human implications reproducible and ethically grounded.

## Planning checklist

- Confirm the targeted IMWUT issue and current journal submission rules.
- Report deployment context, sensing/data provenance, privacy, consent, and limitations.
- Keep core contribution and evidence understandable without unverified external artifacts.
