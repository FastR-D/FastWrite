# CHI 2027 Papers

## Hard constraints

- Use the required one-column ACM submission format (`acmart` manuscript/review; use `anonymous` for blind submission); use the publication workflow and two-column form only after conditional acceptance.
- Submissions are anonymized. Names, affiliations, acknowledgements, supplements, and external dataset/code links must not reveal identity; violations can be desk-rejected.
- Papers should normally be 5,000--8,000 words; submissions above 12,000 words are desk-rejected unless strongly justified. Important contribution evidence must be in the main PDF.
- Submit original, distinct work not under concurrent review, and follow ACM policy for human participants, permissions, authorship, and publication ethics.

## Writing and review priorities

- Make the HCI problem, prior-work comparison, method, evidence, and contribution proportional to length.
- Report participant/data ethics, accessibility, limitations, and contextual boundaries honestly.

## Planning checklist

- Run a word-count and anonymity audit including supplements, metadata, and external links.
- Ensure the main paper stands alone rather than relying on appendices.
- Map participant protection, data handling, and accessibility decisions to the relevant sections.
