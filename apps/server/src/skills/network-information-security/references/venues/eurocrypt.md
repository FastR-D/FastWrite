# EUROCRYPT 2027

This file records the official EUROCRYPT 2027 main-conference rules verified on the date in the frontmatter. They are edition-specific; recheck the official submission page for any later edition or camera-ready update.

## Hard constraints

- Submit an anonymous PDF prepared in LaTeX2e using the current Springer LNCS format. Use `\documentclass{llncs}` and do not use packages that alter the default font or layout; keep default margins, spacing, and font.
- Show page numbers in the submission (for example, `\pagestyle{plain}`).
- Limit the submission to **27 pages excluding references**. Clearly marked supplementary material may follow the main paper, but reviewers are not required to read it; the paper must remain intelligible and technically complete without it.
- Keep the final proceedings version to **30 pages total** under the standard Springer format.
- Preserve anonymity: omit author names, affiliations, and obvious identifying references. Author identities are entered separately in HotCRP and hidden from reviewers.
- Start with a title, short abstract, and introduction. Do not concurrently submit substantially overlapping work to a journal or venue with published proceedings.
- Use the current official conflict-of-interest policy in HotCRP and provide all required author ORCIDs. The call warns that incomplete COI information can lead to rejection.
- Before submission, real-time verify the deadline, HotCRP availability, current AI-use policy, author-attestation requirements, and the exact LNCS package version.

## Writing and review priorities

- Make the introduction understandable to a general cryptographic audience: state the problem, the precise result, its assumptions, and its relation to relevant work.
- Put the core technical argument, key proof ideas, constructions, bounds, attacks, or reductions in the main body rather than relying on supplementary material.
- Separate formal guarantees from heuristic, implementation, or empirical claims, and state all models and assumptions exactly.
- Cite related cryptographic work precisely, including DOIs where available. The official call recommends `splncs04.bst`, BibTeX, and CryptoBib.
- Favor depth and correctness over broad but unsupported applicability; use supplementary material for details that genuinely are nonessential to reviewing the central contribution.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Reserve the 27-page budget for the full argument, not just a high-level overview.
- [ ] Ensure the abstract and introduction explain contributions and prior-work positioning to non-specialist cryptographers.
- [ ] Check LNCS class, default layout, page numbering, bibliography style, and final page count.
- [ ] Blind author metadata, acknowledgements, repository history, and self-references while keeping related-work comparison substantive.
- [ ] Place all proof-critical material needed to assess correctness in the main submission.
- [ ] Recheck the live official CFP for author list, ORCID, COI, AI-use, and camera-ready requirements.
