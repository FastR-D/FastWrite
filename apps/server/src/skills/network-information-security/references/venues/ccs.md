# ACM CCS 2026

This file records the CCS 2026 main-conference rules verified on the date in the frontmatter. The conference uses a two-cycle, multi-track review process. Recheck the official call for a later edition, a different cycle, or camera-ready preparation.

## Hard constraints

- Submit a double-column PDF in the unaltered ACM `sigconf` two-column format. Do not change its margins, fonts, spacing, author block, ACM rights/metadata fields, or use space-saving tricks.
- Keep the main paper at **12 pages or fewer**. The bibliography, clearly marked appendices, and supplementary material are excluded; reviewers need not read them.
- Preserve double-blind review: omit names, affiliations, acknowledgements, funding, identifying self-references, and deanonymizing repository links. Refer to prior work in the third person where possible.
- Register the definitive title, abstract, track, and non-empty track justification before the cycle's registration deadline. The title, abstract, track, and justification cannot subsequently change in HotCRP or the submitted PDF.
- Include a separate `Open Science` appendix after the 12-page main content and bibliography. State where evaluation artifacts are located, or explicitly explain why none are available. Online artifact URLs must be anonymous and be supplied in both paper and HotCRP; the call sets an artifact-availability deadline.
- If the work has potential ethical concerns, include a separate `Ethical Considerations` appendix after the 12-page main content and bibliography. When uncertain, include it.
- Before submission, real-time verify the selected cycle's deadlines, artifact grace period, author/ORCID attestations, track availability, and seven-paper-per-author cap.

## Writing and review priorities

- Establish practical security relevance, a credible threat model, and the exact contribution before mechanism detail.
- Make novelty, technical execution, and evaluation evidence auditable; CCS evaluates importance to practical computer and communications security/privacy, novelty, quality of execution, and presentation.
- Select the track that best represents the paper's primary contribution and write a concise, factual justification. For security/privacy-of-ML submissions, connect the threat model, threat surfaces, generality, practicality, and evaluation directly to the claimed risk.
- Do not present a survey or generic SoK as a CCS main-track paper: CCS 2026 states that it does not accept SoK or survey papers.
- Keep the contribution self-contained in the main body; appendices and supplementary material cannot carry essential evidence needed to assess the claim.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Identify the review cycle and the best-fit CCS track; draft the required track-justification statement.
- [ ] Allocate no more than 12 main-body pages to problem, threat model, design, analysis, evaluation, limitations, and conclusion.
- [ ] Audit the source and artifact links for author identity, institution names, commit history, tracking, acknowledgements, and self-identifying prose.
- [ ] Add `Open Science`; add `Ethical Considerations` whenever the work could pose ethical risk.
- [ ] Verify the ACM `sigconf` template and retained ACM metadata against the official sample immediately before submission.
- [ ] Recheck current-cycle HotCRP registration, ORCID, author-confirmation, artifact, and conflict-of-interest requirements.
