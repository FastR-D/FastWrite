# IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing (TDSC)

## Hard constraints

- Use the current IEEE Computer Society template and IEEE numbered reference style; do not change its page geometry, typography, or bibliography layout.
- Submit original work and disclose/cite any related conference or prior version. A journal extension must make its additional technical substance clear.
- For the regular-paper policy currently published by TDSC, the standard length is 12 double-column pages including references and author biographies; submissions can extend to 18 pages subject to mandatory overlength charges. Short papers are 8 pages and comments are 2 pages. Re-check the live author page when choosing a type because IEEE policies can change.
- Submit supplementary material as separate files, not as pages appended to the main-paper PDF; the current author information states no page limit for supplementary files.
- Follow the single-blind IEEE review workflow and complete required author, ethics, copyright, permissions, and portal declarations.

## Writing and review priorities

- Explain the dependability or security problem, assets, adversary/failure model, assumptions, and operational impact early.
- Demonstrate soundness with rigorous analysis, proofs, attacks, fault models, or measurement evidence appropriate to the claim.
- Evaluate under realistic workloads and failure/adversary conditions; compare fairly with meaningful baselines and report limits.
- Separate what is guaranteed, empirically observed, and assumed. Do not turn a threat-model assumption into a security claim.
- Make the journal extension complete enough to guide future system or security design.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Select regular, short, or comment paper and budget the main PDF with references and biographies included.
- [ ] Validate that the manuscript uses the unmodified current IEEE Computer Society template.
- [ ] State the threat/failure model, trust boundaries, assumptions, evaluation environment, and limitations explicitly.
- [ ] Prepare a clear comparison and disclosure for every related conference or previously reviewed version.
- [ ] Move genuinely supplemental proofs, datasets, traces, or artifacts into separately submitted supplements and verify their references from the main paper.
