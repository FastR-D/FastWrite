# USENIX Security 2027

This file records the USENIX Security 2027 main-track rules verified on the date in the frontmatter. Do not apply the stricter 2026 requirement for a mandatory Ethics appendix: 2027 makes it strongly encouraged, while retaining mandatory Open Science content.

## Hard constraints

- Submit an anonymous PDF using the unaltered USENIX Security template and style files: US letter, two columns, 10-point Times Roman on 12-point leading, in a 7 by 9 inch text block.
- Keep body text to **13 pages or fewer**, excluding references and appendices. Initial submissions may have unlimited references/appendices, but optional appendices are not required reading and the paper must be self-contained. Camera-ready papers are capped at **20 total pages**.
- Include an `Open Science` appendix in every submission. It must say how to access evaluation artifacts or explicitly explain why artifacts cannot be provided. Artifact links must be anonymous, non-tracking, and free of author/institution/commit-history identifiers; the paper URL is final after submission and artifacts have a three-day upload/anonymization grace period.
- Preserve double-blind review: omit author names/affiliations and identifying text. Cite prior work in the third person where feasible; do not use artifact links that reveal identity or track reviewers.
- Register the fixed title, complete author list with ORCIDs, tentative abstract, and fixed topics one week before the submission deadline. Each author must confirm the submission terms by submission time.
- Do not manipulate layout to gain space. The official call prohibits whitespace and formatting changes; satisfy the limit by editing content only.
- Before submission, real-time verify the selected cycle's dates, required HotCRP fields, author cap, artifact deadline, ethics guidance, and the currently linked template.

## Writing and review priorities

- Make the contribution a finished, scientifically rigorous systems-security argument: relevance, correctness, originality, and clarity are explicit review criteria.
- Establish a clear application to security or privacy of computing systems. Purely ML-method work for a security/privacy task is not a fit for the ML topic; frame and tag it by the underlying security problem instead.
- Evaluate systems assumptions, realistic adversaries, deployment constraints, robustness, baselines, and reproducibility in the main body.
- If submitting a systematization-of-knowledge paper, prefix its title with `SoK:` and offer analysis, gaps, limitations, or new insight beyond a summary.
- Include a well-labelled ethics discussion whenever the research or consequences pose nontrivial risk. It is strongly encouraged in 2027 and reviewers evaluate ethics of every submission.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Put the complete contribution and essential evidence within the 13-page body-text budget.
- [ ] Add an `Open Science` appendix that names all necessary artifacts, their anonymous access route, and any justified exclusions.
- [ ] Audit source, PDF metadata, artifact hosting, usernames, organization names, repository history, and tracking for anonymization leaks.
- [ ] Add a concrete ethics section/appendix and assess affected stakeholders, potential harms, safeguards, and disclosure.
- [ ] Check grayscale legibility, template conformance, and the 20-page camera-ready plan.
- [ ] Reconfirm current-cycle registration, ORCID, topic, author-cap, COI, and artifact rules in the official CFP.
