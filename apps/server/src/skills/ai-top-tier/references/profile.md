# AI Top-Tier

Use one shared writing profile for top-tier AI and machine-learning papers. Present a crisp research question, explicit assumptions, a precise problem formulation, and a contribution list whose claims are traceable to theory or experiments.

Explain the method at a level that supports reproduction. Define notation before use and distinguish proposed components from standard machinery. Evaluation should answer concrete research questions with competitive and appropriate baselines, controlled ablations, multiple seeds or uncertainty estimates where relevant, dataset and compute details, robustness checks, and honest negative results. Separate observed performance from causal explanations and avoid implying generalization beyond the evaluated setting.

Discuss related work by technical relationship rather than citation inventory. State limitations, failure modes, compute and data constraints, reproducibility details, ethics, and broader impacts candidly. Prefer concise, calibrated prose and verifiable claims. Never invent metrics, experiments, citations, datasets, or theoretical guarantees.
