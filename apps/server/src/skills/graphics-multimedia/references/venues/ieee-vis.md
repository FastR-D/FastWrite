# IEEE VIS Full Papers

## Hard constraints

- Use the current IEEE VIS/VGTC submission format. The 2026 limit is 9 content pages plus up to 2 pages containing only references and allowed special sections (supplement links, figure credits, acknowledgements); non-reference argument/figures/tables after page 9 risk desk rejection.
- An appendix can follow page 11 under the 2026 policy, but it is additional material rather than a substitute for a self-contained core paper. Extra reference pages require a stated justification and TVCG approval.
- Title, abstract, and complete author list are fixed after the abstract deadline. The 2026 process permits either single-blind or author-chosen double-blind submission; anonymization is strongly recommended where scientifically appropriate, so it is not encoded as an unconditional boolean.

## Writing and review priorities

- Anchor the visualization contribution in the full research context and state of the art; the generous reference allocation is intentional.
- Evaluate analytic/visual claims with appropriate tasks, datasets, users, baselines, uncertainty, and limitations.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Keep main evidence in 9 pages and restrict pages 10–11 to permitted special content.
- [ ] Choose and correctly apply the desired blinding mode; audit metadata and supplements if anonymous.
- [ ] Check figure credits, accessibility, data provenance, and the authenticity of each cited source.
