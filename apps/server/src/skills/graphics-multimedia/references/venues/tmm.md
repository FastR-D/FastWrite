# IEEE Transactions on Multimedia

## Hard constraints

- A regular-paper initial submission may not exceed 10 double-column pages, including title, author information, abstract, text, figures, tables, appendices, proofs, and references. Revised regular papers may use up to 14 pages.
- Prepare a double-column, single-spaced PDF using 10-point type and the current IEEE template. All authors require ORCID records and the manuscript author list must match the submission system.
- Upload non-public cited material for review. TMM supplemental material is limited to four double-column pages unless the current instructions say otherwise.
- Disclose prior rejected versions and related preprints as required; do not submit concurrently.

## Writing and review priorities

- Establish multimedia relevance, technical novelty, complete comparisons, dataset and media provenance, implementation details, uncertainty, failure cases, and reproducibility.
- Keep the main paper self-contained; use supplements for specialist detail, not new unreferenced theorems or essential evidence.
- Verify every reference's DOI/title/year and that it substantiates the associated multimedia claim.

## Planning checklist

- Budget all submission content, including references, within 10 pages for an initial regular paper.
- Check `IEEEtran`, double-column layout, 10-point type, author/ORCID consistency, abstract length and self-containment, EDICS, supplements, and disclosed prior versions.
- Recheck article type and stage because revised papers, overview articles, and comment correspondence use different limits.
