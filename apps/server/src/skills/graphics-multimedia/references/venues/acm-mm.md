# ACM Multimedia Technical Track

## Hard constraints

- Submit a double-blind ACM-format PDF with 6–8 pages of content plus at most 2 references-only pages. Do not append material to the main PDF; supplementary material is separate (2026 limit: 50 MB).
- Preserve anonymity in the paper and supplement: remove names, affiliations, acknowledgements/grant IDs, self-identifying phrases, and identifying links. Wrong template/length or broken blinding can be desk rejected.
- Original non-concurrent work is required. The 2026 author list is fixed after abstract deadline; all authors need complete OpenReview profiles and accept the reviewing obligation.
- Respect ACM policies on plagiarism, authorship, AI use, and hidden prompts. The 2026 call caps an author at 10 submissions across specified tracks.

## Writing and review priorities

- Show a genuinely multimedia/multimodal contribution where applicable, not a unimodal result relabelled as multimedia.
- Provide appropriate datasets, modalities, baselines, evaluation and responsible-multimedia discussion; state limitations and reproducibility details.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Keep all argument, figures, and tables within 6–8 content pages; keep reference pages references-only.
- [ ] Audit all supplemental files, metadata, URLs, visual credits and PDFs for identity leakage.
- [ ] Verify every cited dataset/method/result and check that all claims have empirical or analytical support.
