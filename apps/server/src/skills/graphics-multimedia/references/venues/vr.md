# IEEE VR Papers

## Hard constraints

- Use the IEEE Computer Society VGTC conference format and submit PDF. The 2026 paper body (text, figures, tables, and appendices) is 4–9 pages; references may not exceed 2 pages. Supplemental material is separate.
- The 2026 review process is unified for TVCG and conference-only outcomes; all senior PhD-holding authors are expected to volunteer as reviewers unless already serving.
- Recheck the active call for blinding, artifact, ethics, and camera-ready changes; the current source does not make one review-mode field universally applicable.

## Writing and review priorities

- Every paper must evaluate its contribution through suitable user studies, benchmarking, and/or comparisons with existing techniques/systems.
- Explain immersive task/context, apparatus, latency/system details, participant methods where applicable, and limitations/safety implications.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Keep all appendices inside the 4–9 body-page budget; references within their separate two-page maximum.
- [ ] Verify the final VGTC layout against sample PDFs and inspect figures for accessibility/readability.
- [ ] Validate user-study claims, baselines, data, and citations against primary evidence.
