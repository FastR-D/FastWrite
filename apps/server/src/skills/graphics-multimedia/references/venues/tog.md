# ACM Transactions on Graphics

## Hard constraints

- Use the live TOG/ACM author instructions and production template. Do not transfer annual SIGGRAPH track limits to ordinary TOG submissions.
- Follow ACM originality, authorship, generative-AI disclosure, attribution, copyright/permission, artifact, and required source-file policies.
- Current page budget and review-mode details must be verified in the active journal workflow; leave them `uncertain` when no official article-type confirmation is available.

## Writing and review priorities

- Journal work should be novel, comprehensively described, and thoroughly validated for the claimed graphics/interactive-technique advance.
- Supply reproducible implementation/evaluation details and accurate credit for data, code, figures, and visual assets.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Check active TOG article type, current template, length, and supplementary-material requirements.
- [ ] Audit all externally sourced visual material for permissions and figure credits.
- [ ] Validate every method/dataset/result citation against a primary source.
