# ACM TODS

## Hard constraints

- Follow the current TODS/ACM author instructions, template, disclosure, originality, copyright, and data/code policies; journal requirements can change outside conference cycles.
- TODS uses double-blind review; prepare the review manuscript without identifying material while keeping necessary related work accurately cited.
- Do not encode a page limit without a current journal instruction; flag it `uncertain` and verify it in the submission system.

## Writing and review priorities

- Present a mature database contribution with formal or systems evidence, complete experimental methodology, and clear novelty over prior work.
- Make claims reproducible and cite original systems, algorithms, data, and the exact comparison version.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Reopen current TODS author guidelines before submission and revision.
- [ ] Check anonymous manuscript, supplement, source/PDF metadata, and acknowledgements.
- [ ] Resolve every citation to a genuine publication and identify unsupported comparative claims.
