# ACM TOIS

## Hard constraints

- Use the current TOIS/ACM author-guideline page and template; do not infer a fixed page limit or review mode from a past issue.
- Comply with ACM policies on originality, attribution, authorship, AI disclosure, permissions, and data/code statements.
- Treat missing current submission-format information as `uncertain` and require a final author-portal check.

## Writing and review priorities

- Anchor the work in information retrieval/information systems and make task, users/data, relevance assessments, baselines, and evaluation protocol inspectable.
- Support empirical claims with reproducible details and distinguish statistical association from causal claims.
