# VLDB / PVLDB Research Track

## Hard constraints

- Follow the official PVLDB template and submission system. Volume 20 regular research papers are limited to 12 pages excluding references; use the active category's rules rather than applying this limit to all tracks.
- Abstract registration by the 25th of the preceding month, a two-paper monthly cap, and a twelve-paper yearly per-author cap apply to Volume 20 research submissions; violations can be desk rejected.
- EA&B submissions must provide the required reproducibility package at initial submission. A rejected research-track paper cannot be resubmitted for 12 months.

## Writing and review priorities

- Classify the contribution correctly (regular research, scalable data science, EA&B, or vision) and meet that category's evidence standard.
- Give a reproducible workload, fair comparisons, resource costs, and a clear explanation of practical database significance.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Confirm the current volume, category, monthly deadline, author-cap status, and camera-ready format.
- [ ] Reserve space for methodology, workload/data provenance, baselines, resource use, and limitations.
- [ ] Validate artifact links and bibliography identifiers; retain evidence that every citation exists and supports its claim.
