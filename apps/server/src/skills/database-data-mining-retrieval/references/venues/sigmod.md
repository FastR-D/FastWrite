# SIGMOD Research Track

## Hard constraints

- Use the active call's ACM two-column template and submit the required PDF/source artifacts. The 2026 Research Track is double-anonymous; remove author, affiliation, acknowledgement, self-identifying repository, PDF metadata, and supplemental-material leaks.
- The work must be original and not simultaneously under review. Follow ACM authorship, disclosure, plagiarism, and generative-AI policies.
- The annual track, page budget, author-feedback rules, artifact policy, and camera-ready requirements are dynamic; retrieve the current official call before declaring them satisfied.

## Writing and review priorities

- Make the database problem, workload, data model, system assumptions, correctness and scalability evidence explicit.
- Compare with appropriate state-of-the-art systems/algorithms under reproducible configurations; explain limits and trade-offs.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Verify the active Research/Industrial/Demo track, template, exact page limit, and annual dates.
- [ ] Put the core contribution and essential evidence in the reviewer-required body, not only in supplementary material.
- [ ] Audit anonymity and verify every cited system, result, dataset, and DOI against primary sources.
