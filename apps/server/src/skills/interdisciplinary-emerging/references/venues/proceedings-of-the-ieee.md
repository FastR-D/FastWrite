# Proceedings of the IEEE

## Hard constraints

- Regular papers must be broad survey, review, or tutorial contributions with novel insight and perspective; transactions-style reports of primarily new results and superficial or niche surveys are out of scope.
- Submit a detailed cover letter explaining broad importance, coverage type, difference from prior surveys/reviews/tutorials, author qualifications, and background citations beyond the authors' work.
- Use the IEEE transactions template. There is no hard page limit, but the official guidance says regular papers are typically 20--25 pages and strongly recommends no more than 35 pages.
- Include author photos and biographies (each biography no more than 200 words); comply with IEEE disclosure, plagiarism, duplicate-submission, permission, and AI-generated-text requirements.

## Writing and review priorities

- Explain a technology comprehensively for a broad technical audience, critically assess evidence, and develop future directions.
- Synthesize rather than list literature; distinguish review evidence from the authors' original interpretation.

## Planning checklist

- Prepare the required cover letter and validate broad-audience fit before drafting.
- Budget roughly 20--25 IEEE-template pages and explicitly justify exceptional length.
- Include only verifiable citations and disclose AI-generated text as required by the live IEEE policy.
