# RTSS 2026

## Hard constraints

- Use the IEEE conference template. The main body is at most 11 pages of technical content; acknowledgements and bibliography may follow. Accepted final versions have at most 12 technical pages.
- Follow TCRTS double-anonymous requirements for the PDF and all supplemental material.
- Submit original work that explicitly addresses real-time requirements or constraints. A workshop paper with a DOI needs at least 30% new material.
- Use the submission system's supplementary-material field instead of external repositories; supplementary archives must be anonymous and are not a way to evade page limits.
- Comply with IEEE rules on AI-generated text and TCRTS transparency policy.

## Writing and review priorities

- Explicitly state timing requirements, assumptions, workload/environment, and how the method establishes or evaluates predictability.
- Support theory, design, implementation, verification, or empirical evidence at the technical depth required by the claimed timing guarantee.

## Planning checklist

- Put timing constraints and scope on page one; exclude non-real-time framing.
- Budget the complete technical argument in 11 pages.
- Blind supplements and PDF metadata, then verify generated-text disclosures under the active IEEE policy.
