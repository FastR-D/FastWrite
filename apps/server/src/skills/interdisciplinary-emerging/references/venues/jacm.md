# Journal of the ACM

## Hard constraints

- Submit original work using the current ACM journal submission workflow and template; follow current ACM authorship, publication ethics, permissions, and research-with-human-participants policies where applicable.
- The official ACM-wide author source does not state a JACM-wide hard length limit, anonymous-review rule, or journal-specific LaTeX token. Confirm current JACM portal instructions before enforcing any of those constraints.

## Writing and review priorities

- Make a durable computer-science contribution with precise statements, complete supporting argument, and accurate positioning against prior work.
- Preserve enough detail for an expert to scrutinize proofs, algorithms, empirical methods, or cross-disciplinary implications.

## Planning checklist

- Confirm article type, live format, and review workflow before locking the outline.
- Trace each central claim to proof or empirical evidence in the manuscript.
- Check originality, attribution, and permission status for all reused material.
