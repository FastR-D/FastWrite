# Science China Information Sciences

## Hard constraints

- Submit original work not previously published or under consideration elsewhere; all named authors must approve the submitted version and authorship changes require written agreement.
- Choose the article type: research papers are no more than 15 printed pages; letters no more than 3 printed pages. Reviews average 20 printed pages rather than having a stated hard cap.
- Use the current journal submission service and supply the required text, image/table files, author information, and copyright materials when accepted.
- The verified author information does not establish a fixed anonymous-review or LaTeX-template constraint; do not infer one without checking the live journal system.

## Writing and review priorities

- Present new, original, significant results for broad information-science readership with concise and reliable essential evidence.
- Reviews should analyze the state of the art and future directions rather than enumerate papers.

## Planning checklist

- Select research paper, review, MOOP, or letter before budgeting pages.
- Keep research papers within 15 printed pages and letters within 3; verify final typesetting with the editorial office if near the boundary.
- Confirm authorship consent and disclose related submissions before upload.
