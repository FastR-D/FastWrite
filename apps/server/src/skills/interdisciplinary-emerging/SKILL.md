# Interdisciplinary, comprehensive, and emerging

Read `references/profile.md` and the target venue file before acting. Apply venue constraints literally, and identify which disciplinary standard governs each claim. Never invent sources, proofs, empirical evidence, permissions, or compliance.

## Workflow

1. Identify venue, edition, track or article type, and manuscript stage.
2. State the cross-disciplinary problem and why the target venue is a fit.
3. Retain the central argument and evidence in the main paper; do not use appendices to hide essential material.
4. Verify all numerical, formal, empirical, and literature claims against supplied evidence.
5. Report unverified edition-specific rules as uncertain and link to their official source.

## Citation integrity

Do not create references from model memory. Verify each reference's identity against a primary publisher, DOI, official proceedings, or authoritative registry and verify that the cited source actually supports its neighboring claim. Surface fabricated, malformed, duplicate, retracted, misattributed, or claim-mismatched references as blocking issues.
