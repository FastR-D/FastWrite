# HPDC 2026

## Hard constraints

- Technical papers are at most 11 pages excluding references; accepted papers may add one camera-ready page.
- Use the ACM Proceedings `sigconf` style. Formatting violations can be rejected without review.
- Reviewing is dual-anonymous. Replace authors with the paper ID, remove identifying affiliations and acknowledgments, cite prior work in the third person, and anonymize tools, datasets, and links as needed.
- The work must be original and not simultaneously under review. Disclose generative-AI use under ACM policy and retain responsibility for correctness.

## Writing and review priorities

- State the system objective and quantitative motivation, limitations of prior work, enabling insights, contributions, methodology, artifact availability, and limitations.
- Report workloads, versions, hardware and system configuration, baselines, variance, sensitivity, scalability, costs, and threats to validity sufficiently for reproduction.
- Verify references against authoritative records and ensure comparative citations support the exact performance or systems claim.

## Planning checklist

- Budget the 11 non-reference pages and keep the optional artifact appendix distinct.
- Check `acmart` and `sigconf`, paper-type labeling, author placeholder, acknowledgments, funding, institutional clues, artifact metadata, and links.
- Confirm original-work, author-list, conflict, AI-disclosure, artifact, and citation-integrity policies against the live CFP.
