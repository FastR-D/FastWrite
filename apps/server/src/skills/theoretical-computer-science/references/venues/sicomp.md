# SIAM Journal on Computing

## Hard constraints

- Use the current SIAM/SICOMP submission instructions, article template, disclosure, copyright, and originality policies. The live workflow is the authority for page budget and review mode.
- Do not present generated text or an AI system as an author; disclose any use when required by the active SIAM policy.
- Treat page and template details unavailable from the official current instruction as `uncertain`, not as hard-coded legacy rules.

## Writing and review priorities

- Provide precise algorithms/theorems, complete proofs, clarity about model and complexity, and a careful relation to prior results.
- Favor insight and rigorous verification over unsupported broad impact language.
