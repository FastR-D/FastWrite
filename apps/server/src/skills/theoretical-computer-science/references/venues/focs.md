# FOCS

## Hard constraints

- The latest official format located (FOCS 2025) has lightweight double-blind review and no page limit. Use a complete-paper PDF, 11-point-or-larger single-column text, 1-inch margins, US-letter paper, and place a clear merit/key-ideas presentation within the first ten pages after the title page.
- Supply proofs enabling verification of main claims; material other than abstract, references, and first ten pages may be read at committee discretion.
- The active annual call must be checked before submission for dates, system, policy changes, and final instructions. Preserve required references despite anonymity.

## Writing and review priorities

- Explain new original theory, significance, and conceptual insight for a broad TCS readership.
- State all model, computational, and asymptotic assumptions and make proof dependencies inspectable.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Verify current FOCS CFP before treating 2025 rules as binding.
- [ ] Make the first ten pages self-contained enough to establish merit.
- [ ] Check all theorem citations and preprint/publication status.
