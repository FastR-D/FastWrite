# SODA

## Hard constraints

- SODA 2027 uses lightweight double-blind review: omit author identities and obvious self-identifying references, but do not weaken the paper or omit important citations; preprints and talks remain permitted under the stated policy.
- There is no page limit. Submit a PDF with a title page and 1–2 paragraph abstract; use 11-point-or-larger single-column, single-spaced text, 1-inch margins, and US-letter paper.
- The first ten pages after the title page must clearly explain merit, importance, and key ideas. Provide proofs sufficient to verify main mathematical claims; material after the first ten pages may be read at the committee's discretion.
- Follow SIAM AI-use disclosure, originality, and simultaneous-submission rules.

## Writing and review priorities

- Address a broad theory/discrete-mathematics audience and demonstrate new insight for the design, use, or analysis of efficient algorithms.
- Present formal claims with complete verification and position them precisely against prior work.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Keep abstract, first ten pages, and complete proofs mutually consistent.
- [ ] Put references at the end and use full author surnames rather than `et al.` where the call encourages it.
- [ ] Verify every theorem attribution and source before submission.
