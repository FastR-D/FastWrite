# STOC

## Hard constraints

- Obtain the active STOC CFP and submission template before finalizing: its exact deadline, review mode, page/format requirements, and publication rules are annual and are not encoded without current official confirmation.
- Submit only original, non-concurrently reviewed work and comply with ACM authorship, disclosure, attribution, and conflict policies.
- Do not omit essential references or proof material merely to improve anonymity; apply the active call's balance between blinding and comprehensibility.

## Writing and review priorities

- Lead with the formal problem, theorem, relation to prior bounds, and the conceptual/technical advance.
- Make proofs, reductions, and assumptions independently checkable; assess novelty, correctness, significance, and clarity.

## Planning checklist

- [ ] Recheck the current STOC CFP and template in the official SIGACT site.
- [ ] Put theorem statements, key ideas, and enough proof detail in the reviewer-read portion.
- [ ] Verify every named theorem and bibliographic reference against a primary source.
