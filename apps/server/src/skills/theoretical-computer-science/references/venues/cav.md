# CAV

## Hard constraints

- Use LNCS format. CAV 2026 Regular Papers are anonymized and limited to 18 pages excluding references and appendices; reviewers need not read appendices.
- The other 2026 categories have different limits and review modes: Short Tool Papers (10, not anonymous), Short Application Papers (10, anonymous), and Industrial Experience/Case Studies (10, not anonymous). Choose the matching track before applying a rule.
- Work must not be published or simultaneously submitted to another archival conference; follow Springer guidance on AI authorship. Camera-ready regular papers are up to 20 pages excluding bibliography and data-availability statement.

## Writing and review priorities

- Connect formal foundations, algorithms/tools, and practical verification evidence. State specifications, guarantees, limitations, and evaluation benchmarks precisely.
- Include artifact intent and reproducibility details when applicable.

- [ ] Select the category and apply its exact anonymity/page rule.
- [ ] Keep essential theorem/tool evidence in the main paper; label appendix-only detail.
